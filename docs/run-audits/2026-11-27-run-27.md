### NOEMA-007

**FACT:** WMO's 2026 global status page reports 136 countries with MHEWS as of August 2026, a 70% headline, a 143% increase since 2015, and fewer than half of reporting countries with all four pillars.  
**INFERENCE:** system existence and national capability cannot be used as person-level warning coverage or verified action.  
**FACT:** WMO's summary describes an association between more comprehensive systems and lower mortality/fewer affected people; no causal estimate is inferred.
