## AquaSignal — NOEMA-2026-014

**FACT:** Government Decree №447/2026 Articles 3-7 specify annual plan submission, standardized data/access, representative and risk-based monitoring, five monitoring programme categories, three monitoring modes, and groundwater-specific prerequisites. Article 3(2)(a) sets the annual plan submission-to-Minister deadline at 1 December. Article 7(2) requires an abstraction-data-based water balance before quantitative groundwater status assessment.

**INFERENCE:** Plan existence, submission, approval, annex inspection, monitoring execution and water-body status must be separate evidence objects.

**HYPOTHESIS:** A provenance-first intake can reduce false positive claims of plan completion or legal compliance.

## NOEMA-007 — NOEMA-2026-015

**FACT:** UNDRR's 2026-10-13 article reports that about four in five disaster-impacted respondents received an advance warning, while 64% of warned respondents said they could act. Its per-100 graphic shows approximately 51 warned-and-able, 29 warned-but-unable and 20 not warned.

**REPRODUCED RESULT:** From the frozen one-decimal PROJWT-weighted WRP aggregate (80.0% warning receipt; 63.8% ability among warned), the Decimal derivation produces 51.0/29.0/20.0, summing to 100.0. This is arithmetic on rounded aggregates, not a raw-microdata reproduction or a new survey.

**INFERENCE:** The shared denominator makes visible that successful warning delivery is not equivalent to ability to act.

**LIMITS:** No design-based confidence intervals, causal effects or Georgia national estimates are produced. The components are approximate because the inputs were rounded; residual rounding is allocated to the warned-but-unable category.
