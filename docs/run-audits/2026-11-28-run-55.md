## NOEMA-007 — REPRODUCED RESULT and interpretation boundary

The WRP 2025 data dictionary's `CountryIncomeLevel` is a country-level World Bank classification at fieldwork (FY2024-25), not respondent/household personal income.

From the frozen PROJWT-weighted point estimates:
- Three vs one warning channel: +26.7 percentage points.
- Both household plan + personal agency vs neither: +56.4 pp.
- Upper-middle vs low country-income group: +27.5 pp.
- High vs lower-middle country-income group: +24.0 pp.

These are unadjusted differences between weighted point estimates. No design-based confidence intervals, effective subgroup sample sizes or causal effects were computed. They do not establish that adding channels or plans causes an individual to act. The study remains Preliminary.

- 6/6 embedded public JSON SHA-256 digests matched.

**LIMITS:** the six cells are GitHub-hosted virtual runners, not physical-device or independently administered replication. CPU frequency, context switches and thermal state were not measured. No wall-time acceleration is demonstrated.
