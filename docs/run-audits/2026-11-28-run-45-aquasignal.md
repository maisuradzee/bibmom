- **Primary source:** Matsne Government Decree №447, adopted 2026-09-15, effective for relations arising from 2026-09-01. Official HTML Articles 3–7 reviewed. Cross-checked the consolidated Water Resources Management Law (amendment published 2026-09-16), Articles 31, 37 and 42.
- **Key finding:** annual plan submission is due by 1 December; the 2026-12-01 deadline is upcoming, not overdue. State monitoring is representative/risk-based and includes five programme families. Microbiological monitoring is selected by water-body type, pressures, use and microbial risk. At-risk operational monitoring is monthly/quarterly; investigative monitoring is incident- or unknown-cause-driven.
- **Matrix finding:** biota/sediment monitoring is progressively introduced; until full implementation, water-column results may mainly support chemical-status assessment. For substances requiring those matrices, the latest start date is 2028-01-01.
- **Legal boundary:** Decree 447 is a state water-resource monitoring rule for water bodies, not a complete consumer tap-water compliance regulation. The consolidated Law assigns drinking-water quality state control to NFA and sets a separate 2027-09-01 technical-regulation milestone.
