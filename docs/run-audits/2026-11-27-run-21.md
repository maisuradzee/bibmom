### New source-context finding

The UNECE/WHO PDF is a Government of Georgia submission dated 2025-04-01. Its executive summary says Georgia is not a Party to the Protocol and did not set Protocol target indicators for 2022–2024. It must therefore be described as government-submitted reporting, not as an independent UNECE/WHO survey.

The report identifies NFA as the drinking-water control body and describes sample collection at head facilities, treatment-facility outlets and consumer intake points. However:
- the specific sample-source field for the indicator tables is blank;
- the E. coli table denominator and urban/rural rows are blank;
- the report says small rural systems were included in the 2024 laboratory-control programme and attributes the reported 2024 increase to that inclusion.

The JECM 2023 broad non-compliance value (49.5%) and the government-submitted UNECE/WHO narrative value (44.5%) remain numerically unreconciled. The two are conceptually similar broad indicators, but the available material does not establish identical sample frames or counting rules. The 2024 E. coli table and the broader narrative both show 40.3%; equal values do not establish identical indicator definitions.
