### AquaSignal — Decree №447 deadline semantics

Primary source: [Government Decree №447/2026, Matsne](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/7000572).

- **FACT:** Article 3(2)(a) requires the National Environmental Agency to submit the annual State Water Resources Monitoring Plan to the Minister for approval no later than 1 December.
- **FACT:** Article 3(2)(c) separately provides access to monitoring data subject to environmental-information, data-protection and confidentiality law.
- **Interpretation boundary:** the 1 December deadline is for submission to the Minister; the cited text does not establish a public-release deadline of 1 December.
- **As-of state:** 2026-11-28, the deadline was upcoming. Submission evidence was not verified. This is not evidence of non-submission and no legal breach is inferred.
- **Annex limitation:** the Matsne page lists a DOCX attachment. Its download endpoint returned `application/msword`, which the available reader could not inspect. No annex tables, frequencies or parameter rows are asserted.

It never converts missing public evidence into a non-submission or legal-compliance finding. Reported submission dates require a bounded HTTPS provenance URL without userinfo, query or fragment.

### INFRA-001 — bounded verified artifact staging

PR #268 added a mandatory caller-supplied `max_staged_byte_length` to `open_verified_artifact()`.

- Invalid/non-positive/bool limits are rejected.
- Expected size above the caller's cap is rejected before opening the source.
- The streaming loop checks both expected length and the policy cap.
- Short writes fail closed.
- The architecture document states that the cap must come from trusted local recovery policy.

This limits temporary staging resource use. It does **not** make `TemporaryFile` memory-only, encrypt staged plaintext, guarantee secure erasure, authenticate the manifest, decrypt ciphertext, verify remote retention, or complete a restore. No production restore consumer is yet wired to this helper.
