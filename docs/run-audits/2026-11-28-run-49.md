## AquaSignal — NOEMA-2026-009

### Primary-source findings

1. WHO, *Guidelines for drinking-water quality*, consolidated fourth edition with addenda, publication date 2026-06-17, nitrate/nitrite chemical fact sheet printed pp. 466–472. It gives 50 mg/L as NO3-, 3 mg/L as NO2- and a combined same-drinking-water-sample ratio. The fact sheet itself states an assessment date of 2016; the 2026 consolidated publication is not represented as a new nitrate/nitrite reassessment.
2. Georgian Government Decree №58/2014, Article 1(2)–(3), Article 2 Table 4 and Article 2(21): for water within scope, nitrate is 50 mg/L as NO3- with a short-term exposure limiting sign; nitrite is 0.2 mg/L as NO2- with a long-term exposure limiting sign. Article 2(21) describes sum-of-ratios treatment for chemicals normalized under the same limiting sign.

### Evidence classification

- **FACT:** source-published WHO values, rounded conversion factors (0.226 nitrate ion to nitrate-nitrogen; 0.304 nitrite ion to nitrite-nitrogen), Georgian values, limiting-sign labels and Article 2(21) wording.
- **INFERENCE — legal review required:** because Georgian Table 4 gives nitrate and nitrite different limiting signs, the text does not support automatically importing WHO's combined ratio as a Georgian legal pass/fail rule. This is a preliminary textual interpretation, not legal advice.
- **REPRODUCED RESULT:** synthetic software tests only; no real water sample was analysed.
- **UNTESTED IDEA:** apply the typed helper in shadow mode after authorized paired laboratory data and legal review.

### Prototype and controls

Added `noema_core/nitrate_nitrite.py`, a strict crosswalk JSON/schema, 14 unit tests, four schema/privacy tests and preliminary record NOEMA-2026-009. The helper requires one pseudonymous sample ID, one timezone-aware collection time, the drinking-water matrix, mg/L and native NO3-/NO2- ion bases. It emits a numeric WHO ratio only; it does not emit Georgian compliance, potability or health-risk status.

No sample-level records, private well-register fields, exact coordinates or personal identifiers were included.

## INFRA-001 — verified artifact staging

Added `open_verified_artifact()` to `noema_core/backup_manifest.py`. It opens one regular-file descriptor, streams bytes into an anonymous TemporaryFile while hashing, checks exact size/digest and before/after descriptor identity/metadata, and returns a rewound handle containing the bytes that were verified.

Nine focused tests cover exact-byte reads, path replacement after open (fail closed), path replacement after verification, in-place source mutation after verification, digest/length mismatch, invalid arguments, directories and symlinks where O_NOFOLLOW exists.

The first PR head failed one test because the test expected a path replacement during hashing to return bytes. The code correctly rejected the changed source metadata. The test was changed to require fail-closed behavior; the corrected exact head passed.

Limitations: no restore consumer is wired to the new handle; expected digest provenance remains caller-owned; TemporaryFile may be disk-backed and is not encryption/secure erase; no clean-host or offsite recovery was performed.
