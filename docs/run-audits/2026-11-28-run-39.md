### AquaSignal operational-control crosswalk — Run 39

The official Matsne text of Government Decree №58/2014 was mapped into a closed, metadata-only operational control record. It distinguishes the legal sampling point for a distribution-network consumer tap, tank/cistern outlet, bottled/container filling and retail points, and food-production water-supply points (Article 1(5)). It also records supplier monitoring/recording/information duties and non-compliance response (Articles 1(6)-(7)), enhanced monitoring during floods/emergencies (Article 3(4)), repeat sampling and chloride/nitrate/nitrite follow-up after coliform/E. coli findings (Article 3(6)), routine analytes (Article 3(9)) and accredited independent laboratory testing (Article 4(2)). This is a legal-text mapping, not evidence of actual supplier performance or water-quality results. Raw groundwater is not automatically a consumer-point compliance sample. The 2026-06-24 amendment's 2027-09-01 milestones mean the transition/repeal status of Decree №58/2014 requires legal review before operational compliance use.
