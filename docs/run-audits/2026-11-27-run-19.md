## DISCOVERY — NOEMA-007

**FACT:** UNDRR, IFRC, ITU and WMO's AER methodological guidance (published 15 July 2026) describes structured, learning-oriented review of monitoring/prediction, forecasts, warning dissemination, communication, coordination and response. Official source: https://www.undrr.org/publication/documents-and-publications/after-event-review-aer-methodological-guidance-conducting

**FACT:** The joint mobile-populations operational guidance (15 July 2026) includes people temporarily present at routes, workplaces and transit hubs, and people unable to move; it treats mobility inclusion as part of the existing multi-hazard warning system. Official source: https://www.undrr.org/publication/documents-and-publications/early-warning-systems-mobile-populations-operational

**INFERENCE:** Resident-only administrative frames may miss people exposed during an event window. Time-location/service-point samples can also omit people unable to reach those locations; the protocol therefore keeps frames separate and requires explicit coverage/nonresponse limitations.

**HYPOTHESIS:** A combination of stratified time-location sampling and community-partner aggregate summaries may identify warning-comprehension or action-feasibility gaps not visible in resident-only frames. This has not been tested in Georgia.

The two local KVM reports in PR #87 remain synthetic 96-MiB memory-backed cancellation benchmarks, not physical-hardware replication. I independently parsed both raw JSON files and compared all 15 matching cases:

- Both reports use the same benchmark source SHA-256 (`dd62d21e579c8baf6e1e13587362b48ef9e015d259ae8d90c6264200d56f34ac`), x86-64 KVM guest, cpuset `0-4`, cgroup quota `400000 100000` (4 CPU-equivalents), and five timed repetitions per case.
- Naive serial/OpenMP reductions were correct in 0/5 runs; long-double and full Neumaier-state reductions were correct in 5/5 at 1/2/4/8 workers.
- Full Neumaier-state median speedup changed between run A and B by -12.2% (1 worker), -14.1% (2), -13.6% (4) and -17.5% (8). Long-double speedups changed +1.3% to +3.6% across 2/4/8 workers. Eight workers are oversubscribed against the four-CPU cgroup quota.
- This confirms run-to-run timing sensitivity on one virtualized host. It is not a confidence interval, DRAM-bandwidth measurement, physical-hardware replication or portable performance claim. Effective bytes/time remains algorithm/runtime throughput only.

No new benchmark was run this cycle; the next valid step remains independent physical x86-64/ARM64 hardware and representative workloads.
