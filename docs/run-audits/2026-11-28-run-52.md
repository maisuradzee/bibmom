### AquaSignal — groundwater quantitative readiness

Merged PR [#275](https://github.com/maisuradzee/bibmom/pull/275), head `ac523fc8841f17489cd98c4151266ea31dd4da51`, merge commit `29994967d4669b340c1fd79e8947f4ee9d4530bd`.

Official Matsne Decree №447 Article 7(1)-(2) supports the use of hydrogeological units before groundwater-body designation and the requirement that the responsible agencies prepare a water balance based on abstraction data before the recharge/abstraction balance is assessed for quantitative status.

The metadata-only prototype `noema_core/aquasignal_groundwater.py`:
- uses hydrogeological-unit scope before formal body designation;
- blocks when authority confirmation or abstraction-data basis is absent;
- requires HTTPS provenance and a source SHA-256 in the caller-supplied evidence metadata;
- never assigns a good/poor status and always requires competent-authority review.

The Annex 2 frequency and low-flow sampling rows are recorded only as **SECONDARY_TRANSCRIPTION_ONLY** from Legali.ge. Matsne lists a 46,049-byte DOCX, but its bytes were not retrieved or hashed. No Annex-derived schedule is encoded in the production rule engine.

No actual authority water-balance record, abstraction volume, sample-level groundwater data or private well data were obtained.
