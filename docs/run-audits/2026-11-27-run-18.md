## DISCOVERY

### NOEMA-007 — mobile-inclusive warning review

**FACT:** UNDRR, IFRC, ITU and WMO published the *After-Event Review (AER): Methodological guidance on conducting after-event reviews of Early Warning Systems* on 15 July 2026. The official page describes structured review of monitoring/prediction, forecasts, warning dissemination, communication, coordination and response, with a non-punitive learning orientation. The publication is 76 pages.  
Source: https://www.undrr.org/publication/documents-and-publications/after-event-review-aer-methodological-guidance-conducting

**FACT:** The same four organisations published *Early Warning Systems for mobile populations: Operational guidance* on 15 July 2026. Its scope treats mobility inclusion as part of existing multi-hazard warning systems, not a separate warning system.  
Source: https://www.undrr.org/publication/documents-and-publications/early-warning-systems-mobile-populations-operational

**INFERENCE:** AER can connect NOEMA-007's existing delivery/receipt/comprehension/feasibility/action metrics to role-owned corrective actions. Endpoint receipts are not unique people; delivery, receipt, comprehension, feasibility and verified action must remain separate.

**HYPOTHESIS:** A locally justified time-and-place or community-partner sampling frame may expose warning gaps not visible in resident-only denominators. This has not been tested in Georgia.

### NOEMA-005 — local memory-backed OpenMP rerun

**FACT:** The fixture repeats `[2^53, 1, -2^53]` 4,194,304 times. Each triplet sums exactly to 1; the exact total is 4,194,304.

**REPRODUCED RESULT:** Two separate local processes ran the same 96 MiB array benchmark with one warm-up and five timed repetitions for 15 serial/OpenMP cases. Naive serial/OpenMP reductions were correct in 0/5 repetitions; long-double and Neumaier-state reductions were correct in 5/5 for every worker setting. Actual OpenMP thread counts matched 1/2/4/8.

| Method | Workers | Run A median speedup | Run B median speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| OpenMP long double | 2 | 2.063x | 2.137x |
| OpenMP long double | 4 | 4.108x | 4.203x |
| OpenMP long double | 8 | 3.940x | 3.991x |
| OpenMP Neumaier state | 2 | 2.469x | 2.119x |
| OpenMP Neumaier state | 4 | 4.147x | 3.583x |
| OpenMP Neumaier state | 8 | 4.644x | 3.830x |

Environment: Linux x86-64 KVM guest, five CPUs in cpuset, four-CPU cgroup quota, 32 MiB reported L3, GCC 14.2.0, OpenMP 201511. Eight workers are oversubscribed. Same-container Neumaier speedups varied 13.6% (4 workers) and 17.5% (8 workers) between runs. Effective bytes/time is not a DRAM counter.

**INFERENCE:** The two runs and the earlier hosted result show that one-host speedup figures are environment-sensitive. They do not establish portable scaling.

**UNTESTED IDEA:** Pinned physical x86-64/ARM64 replication, representative scientific data, hardware memory counters and energy measurement.
