### Discovery and source grounding

WHO's updated 2026 *Guidelines for drinking-water quality* (17 June 2026), Chapter 5 (pp. 75–80), distinguishes surveillance from supplier quality control, warns that piped-only surveillance may miss most supplies where piped systems serve a minority, and calls for supply-type-aware surveillance. For dispersed small/community supplies, it describes rolling programmes using stratified-random or cluster sampling. It also says information alone does not lead to improvement: findings need remedial recommendations and follow-up. Official source: https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/wash-documents/water-safety-and-quality/dwq-guidelines-4/gdwq_4ed_with_third-addenda_surveillance.pdf

The current documentary evidence contains two source-reported 2023 percentages:
- JECM 2025 secondary analysis of National Food Agency monitoring data: 49.5%.
- Government of Georgia report submitted through the UNECE/WHO reporting system: 44.5%.

These are not established as the same indicator or sample frame. The government submission is not an independent UNECE/WHO survey. Neither numerator nor denominator, full sampling frame, exact water matrix, standard version, inclusion rule and analytical-method mapping were verified in the material used here. The five-percentage-point difference remains unresolved.

### Engineering

Added:
- `noema_core/water_comparability.py`
- `data/noema-006/water-indicator-observation.schema.json`
- `data/noema-006/2023-water-noncompliance-source-observations.json`
- `tests/test_noema006_water_comparability.py`
- `research/drafts/NOEMA-2026-006-water-indicator-comparability-gate-2026-11-27.md`

The comparator requires identical indicator definition, water matrix, supply-type scope, standard/version, period, geography, sampling frame/design, inclusion rule and analytical method. It also validates numerator/denominator and the source-rounded percentage. Missing metadata yields `INSUFFICIENT_METADATA`; mismatched known fields yield `NOT_COMPARABLE`; absent counts yield `INSUFFICIENT_COUNTS`; a rate/count mismatch yields `SOURCE_RATE_RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`. A successful result is descriptive only and always sets `population_inference_permitted=false`.

The source fixture preserves 49.5% and 44.5% with unknown fields, including supply_type_scope, as null. Unknown supply type is treated as missing metadata, not inferred. It does not compute their difference. The focused test set contains 15 tests and passed on both exact-head workflows. The full suite contained 472 tests with one skipped on both workflows.

### Interpretation and limits

**FACT:** the cited sources publish the two percentages and WHO guidance sets out the surveillance/sampling principles.  
**REPRODUCED RESULT:** synthetic fixtures demonstrate that the gate refuses incomplete metadata and only returns a descriptive comparison when definitions/counts reconcile.  
**INFERENCE:** a metadata-first gate reduces the risk of false trends or municipal rankings from unlike samples.  
**NOT ESTABLISHED:** the true reason for the 49.5%/44.5% discrepancy, a current national compliance rate, municipal risk ranking, population prevalence or water-safety classification.

No raw sample-level, household, health, private-well or operational records were added. No NFA/NEA API or field integration was performed.

Exact-head Quality CI: push [45976110643](https://github.com/maisuradzee/bibmom/actions/runs/45976110643) and pull request [45976114767](https://github.com/maisuradzee/bibmom/actions/runs/45976114767), both SUCCESS.

### Discovery and methodology

The 2026 UNDRR/IFRC/ITU/WMO After-Event Review guidance evaluates monitoring, forecasting, warning dissemination, communication/coordination and response, using a learning-oriented, non-punitive approach: https://www.undrr.org/publication/documents-and-publications/after-event-review-aer-methodological-guidance-conducting

The WMO/UNDRR 2026 MHEWS report shows 136 countries reporting MHEWS but only 64 reporting all four pillars. It identifies disaster-risk knowledge as the weakest pillar (72 of 136) and says effectiveness, behavioural response and protective action need to complement capability counts: https://www.undrr.org/reports/global-status-mhews-2026

These sources justify separating the timing of receipt from receipt itself; they do not establish a Georgian warning-delay problem.

### Engineering

Extended:
- `noema_core/action_readiness.py`
- `data/noema-007/action-readiness-observation.schema.json`
- `tests/test_noema007_action_readiness.py`
- `data/research-portfolio.json`
- `research/protocols/NOEMA-007-timely-receipt-measurement.md`

Six optional, backward-compatible survey fields represent timing-assessed respondents, timely receipts, event-specific minimum lead seconds, measurement basis, rule reference and rule source URL. The validator requires the fields together; timing-assessed respondents cannot exceed self-reported warning receipts; timely receipts cannot exceed timing assessments. Without a paired rule reference/source URL, numerator and denominator are retained but the rate is null. A supplied source is metadata only and is not independently verified. Caller-supplied privacy approval booleans are explicitly labelled as declarations, not authenticated approvals; the validator never authorizes publication. Endpoint receipt counts remain a separate unit and cannot be interpreted as people reached. Legacy records without the fields remain valid and return null rather than zero.

The exact-head CI passed the focused checks and the full Python suite; the current head includes the rule-provenance and approval-declaration follow-up.

### Interpretation and limits

**FACT:** the AER guidance reviews the warning-to-response chain; WMO/UNDRR reports country-level MHEWS capabilities and an effectiveness-measurement gap.  
**INFERENCE:** receipt without timing can hide warnings that arrive too late to support the intended action.  
**HYPOTHESIS:** an event-specific minimum lead-time measure can improve the diagnostic value of action-readiness assessment.  
**UNTESTED:** the new field has not been piloted in Georgia; no actual warning-event, survey or protective-action data were used.

The metric is self-reported, not timestamp-verified. Minimum lead time must be approved by the competent authority for the specific hazard and action playbook. No causal impact, evacuation coverage or avoided-loss claim is made.

- **INFERENCE:** free-threaded workers used aggregate CPU time concurrently, but this Python-heavy workload did not beat the matching serial wall-time baseline.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** physical-device performance, ARM64 performance in this run, DRAM bandwidth, RSS/native allocations, representative scientific workloads or a stable performance distribution.

This is one hosted x86-64 virtual-runner batch, not the requested physical x86-64/ARM64 replication. It uses two synthetic cancellation layouts and three measured repetitions. `tracemalloc` does not measure RSS or native allocations. Status remains Preliminary/Draft.

### AquaSignal count-semantics gate

The initial comparator did not represent the unit and definitions of the counts themselves. A shared indicator ID and sampling frame do not prove that two numerators/denominators count the same unit. The schema now requires `count_unit`, `numerator_definition_id` and `denominator_definition_id`, and the comparator treats missing values as insufficient metadata and mismatches as not comparable. The two Georgian 2023 source observations retain null values for all three fields. Two new synthetic regression tests cover missing and mismatched count semantics. This is a NOEMA conservative engineering control; the WHO 2026 guideline supports supply-type-aware surveillance and designed sampling, but does not prescribe these exact ID fields.

### NOEMA-007 threshold and privacy evidence

The timely-receipt rate is now suppressed (`null`) when no paired event-specific rule reference and HTTPS source URL is supplied. If a source is supplied, the reference is labelled `provided_not_independently_verified`; cross-event comparison remains disabled. Privacy approval booleans are returned as caller declarations, with `publication_authorized_by_validator=false`. The validator cannot authenticate an authority, approval or source document. No Georgian event data or personal/device logs were added.
