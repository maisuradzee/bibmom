## საკვლევი კითხვა

Can the newly announced National Environmental Agency (NEA) 2021-2025 groundwater-monitoring report and its nitrate-risk field methodology provide a traceable baseline for AquaSignal, and what evidence is needed before extracting sample-level or site-level conclusions?

## მოკლე შეჯამება

On 2026-11-26, the NEA announced that its Geological Department had prepared a hydrogeological report covering 2021-2025 monitoring results and observation-network development, plus a field methodology for identifying groundwater contaminated with nitrates or at risk of contamination. The official page links a public RAR archive.

This is a high-value official source-discovery result. The archive was not retrieved or inspected in this run; no sample counts, station counts, nitrate values, thresholds, locations or results are asserted. The source record is metadata-only.

## რატომ არის ეს კითხვა მნიშვნელოვანი?

Groundwater monitoring and nitrate-risk identification can help identify risks before they become persistent exposure problems. A five-year monitoring report may provide temporal and network context that is not available from the NFA's planned 2026 drinking-water sampling allocation. AquaSignal must keep groundwater, distributed drinking water, bottled water and food-control records as separate matrices until the source data dictionary proves comparability.

## არსებული ცოდნა და წყაროები

The NEA announcement says the report covers quantitative and qualitative groundwater characteristics, operation of the automatic monitoring system, processing/digitization of historical hydrogeological information, and development prospects for the observation network. It separately says a practical field methodology was prepared for identifying groundwater contaminated with nitrates or at risk of nitrate contamination.

A Georgian law dated 2026-06-24 sets 2027-09-01 milestones for approval of relevant water-resource-management methodologies and identification of groundwater nitrate-contaminated or at-risk zones. The NEA announcement that a field method was prepared does not prove that the method has been formally approved or that statutory zone identification is complete.

- **FACT:** NEA announcement dated 2026-11-26 confirms preparation of the 2021-2025 hydrogeological report and nitrate-risk field methodology.
- **FACT:** the announcement links a public RAR archive.

- **INFERENCE:** the report may supply a useful baseline for AquaSignal's groundwater-monitoring and nitrate-risk discovery.

- **UNTESTED IDEA:** a provenance-preserving adapter could map approved aggregate monitoring records to network/site identifiers and report only disclosure-reviewed risk summaries.

## კონკურენტი ახსნები

The report may contain only network-level summaries, or may use sampling frames and analytes that cannot be compared directly with NFA's drinking-water samples. A method being prepared does not establish formal adoption. Apparent trends may reflect station changes, method changes, seasonal coverage or historical-data digitization rather than environmental change.

## ჰიპოთეზა

If the report contains time-stamped, quality-controlled, site-linked groundwater observations with a stable data dictionary, then AquaSignal can derive a traceable baseline and identify where nitrate-risk follow-up sampling is most valuable. This hypothesis remains untested until the archive is inspected and its metadata are validated.
