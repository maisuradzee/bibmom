# NOEMA-2026-007 — MHEWS 2026: coverage progress and the warning-to-action gap

- თარიღი: 2026-11-27
- სტატუსი: draft
- Evidence classification: FACT / SOURCE-REPORTED ASSOCIATION / INFERENCE

## საკვლევი კითხვა

Does the presence of a multi-hazard early warning system adequately describe whether people are protected, or must measurement also cover the full chain from hazard knowledge through warning delivery, comprehension and feasible action?

## ძირითადი მიგნებები და მტკიცებულების სტატუსი

1. FACT — WMO MHEWS 2026 reports that 136 countries had multi-hazard early warning systems as of August 2026, a 143% increase since 2015. Fewer than half of reporting countries demonstrated capabilities across all four pillars.
2. REPRODUCED RESULT — the repository's frozen country-level snapshot records 64 of 136 reporting countries with all four pillars, or 47.0588%. This is a country-capability ratio, not population coverage.
3. SOURCE-REPORTED ASSOCIATION — the 2026-11-12 UNDRR/WMO COP31 update reports nearly sixfold lower disaster-related death rates in countries with substantial-to-comprehensive systems than in countries with limited-to-moderate systems. This is a cross-country association, not a causal effect estimate.
4. SOURCE-REPORTED IMPLEMENTATION GAP — the same update reports that four in five people affected by a disaster in the previous five years received advance warning, while more than half of reporting countries still experience a breakdown somewhere between forecast and action.

## მეთოდი ან ექსპერიმენტის გეგმა

This note re-opened the WMO 2026 publication page and the UNDRR/WMO COP31 update on 2026-11-27. It did not independently reprocess country-level MHEWS data or mortality microdata. The existing 64/136 arithmetic is reproduced from the repository's frozen source snapshot. No causal model, Georgia-specific estimate or local operational effectiveness estimate is claimed.

## შეზღუდვები

The nearly sixfold mortality comparison may reflect broader differences in governance, exposure, infrastructure, income, health systems and preparedness. It must not be presented as a causal effect of MHEWS alone. The published pages do not provide the country-level microdata required for independent causal analysis. No Georgian national result is available from these global sources.

## შემდეგი ნაბიჯები

Keep the WRP survey reproduction as global context. Seek data-controller-approved Georgian operational aggregates and pre-register separate denominators for issued, delivered, received, understood, feasible, actioned and independently verified protective action.
