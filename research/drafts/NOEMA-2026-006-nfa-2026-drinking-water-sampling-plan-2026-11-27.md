# NOEMA-2026-006 — NFA 2026 drinking-water sampling plan

- **თარიღი:** 2026-11-27
- **სტატუსი:** draft
- Evidence status: FACT (official annual plan only)

## საკვლევი კითხვა

Can the National Food Agency's 2026 planned drinking-water sampling volume provide a defensible monitoring-planning baseline for AquaSignal, while keeping planned samples separate from actual collection, laboratory results and prevalence denominators?

## ძირითადი მიგნება და მტკიცებულების სტატუსი

- FACT: the NFA's 2026 State Control Programme states that 550 drinking-water samples are planned under its base calculation: K=1.5 multiplied by A=365 gives 547.5, rounded to 550.
- FACT: the programme adds approximately 200 samples for bottled drinking water and states a total planned count of 750 drinking-water samples, including bottled water.
- FACT: the same section says monitoring of the produced volume of bottled drinking water is not planned.
- NOT REPORTED: actual samples collected, actual samples tested, sample-level laboratory results, analyte-specific denominators and geographic distribution are not supplied by this planning passage.
- INFERENCE: 750 is a useful programme-planning target but is not an observed sampling denominator and cannot be used to calculate a compliance or contamination rate.
- UNTESTED IDEA: compare the planned target with actual quarterly collection and testing data once the Agency publishes an authorized completion table.

## მეთოდი ან ექსპერიმენტის გეგმა

The official NFA 2026 State Control Programme PDF was opened and the drinking-water sampling section was inspected on 2026-11-27. The arithmetic 1.5 × 365 = 547.5, rounded to 550, was checked directly against the source. The additional approximately 200 bottled-water samples and total 750 are retained as source-stated planning values. No sample-level data or actual completion is inferred.

## წყაროები

National Food Agency of Georgia, 2026 State Control Programme for Food Safety, official PDF: https://www.nfa.gov.ge/Ge/Files/ViewFile/8866 (accessed 2026-11-27). Relevant lines in the extracted document: 1272–1287.

## შეზღუდვები

The document does not state that 750 samples were actually collected or tested. The planned total combines base drinking-water sampling and an approximate additional bottled-water allocation. It does not provide a produced-bottled-water volume denominator, analyte-specific sampling frames, geographic coverage or test results. It cannot support a prevalence estimate or compliance rate.

## შემდეგი ნაბიჯები

Request the NFA's 2026 actual collection/completion table, broken down by water matrix (distributed network versus bottled), region/municipality, sampling date, analyte, laboratory method, result, compliance threshold and tested-sample denominator. Confirm publication/reuse terms before importing any data.
