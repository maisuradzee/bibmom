# NOEMA-2026-006 — NFA 2026 Tbilisi kindergarten investigation: food/water denominator boundary

- **თარიღი:** 2026-11-27
- **სტატუსი:** draft
- Evidence status: FACT (agency-published investigation update only)

## საკვლევი კითხვა

Can an official public-health investigation update be used as an early-signal source for AquaSignal without conflating combined food-and-water sample counts with water-only results or attributing an outbreak to water without evidence?

## ძირითადი მიგნება და მტკიცებულების სტატუსი

- FACT: on 2026-09-25 the NFA reported that approximately 70 facilities were inspected in a complex investigation concerning norovirus-associated gastroenteritis cases among children in Tbilisi kindergartens.
- FACT: the NFA reported 694 total samples of various food types and drinking water collected for the investigation.
- FACT: the same page separately reported that 129 food samples had been examined and all 129 met the stated standards.
- NOT REPORTED: the source does not split the 694 combined samples into food and drinking-water counts, does not provide water sample results, and says the remaining investigation results would be published gradually.
- INFERENCE: this is a useful operational investigation signal and a clear example of why matrix-specific denominators are necessary.
- NOT ESTABLISHED: the source does not establish that drinking water caused the gastroenteritis cases.

## მეთოდი ან ექსპერიმენტის გეგმა

The official NFA page was opened on 2026-11-27. The 694 combined sample count, approximately 70 inspected facilities, 129 food samples tested and zero reported food-sample violations were transcribed as separate source fields. No water-only count or result was inferred. Child-level information, kindergarten names and exact addresses were excluded.

## წყაროები

National Food Agency of Georgia, “No violations found in 129 food samples taken from kindergartens,” 2026-09-25: https://www.nfa.gov.ge/en/News/Details/3868

## შეზღუდვები

The 694 count combines food and drinking-water samples. The 129 compliant samples are food-only. Water sample count, water analytes, laboratory results and the remaining investigation results were not stated in the retrieved page. The source does not prove a waterborne outbreak, a contamination event, or any population-level water-quality rate.

## შემდეგი ნაბიჯები

Seek the NFA's follow-up investigation update with matrix-specific sample counts, analytes, laboratory methods, results and dates. Preserve separate denominators for food and drinking water. Do not connect the outbreak to water unless the competent public-health authority publishes supporting epidemiological and laboratory evidence.
